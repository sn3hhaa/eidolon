### How the third-party assets are used

- **Protagonist.** From the base avatar the film keeps the head, face rig (blendshape expressions,
  eye bones), hair with its spring bones, the hands and the humanoid skeleton and skin weights.
  Its original outfit is hidden. Her wardrobe — oversized cobalt jacket with orange accents and a
  crossed-out-signal back print, loose cream cargo trousers with thigh pockets, inline skates,
  headphones, crossbody bag — is original and generated by code in `src/char/` and `src/world/props/`.
- **Pedestrians.** The six crowd avatars are used as they are (their own outfits), some with a colour
  tint, downscaled to 1024² textures and instanced.
- **Animation clips.** Pedestrians play retargeted Universal Animation Library clips (walks, idles,
  talking, phone, sitting, a throw, a stumble, a jump). The protagonist's skating is **not** from the
  library: it is authored procedurally in `src/char/skater.ts` and `src/shots/perform*.ts`.

## Made for this film (original, in this repository)

- The city: streets, plazas, buildings, shop fronts and signs (invented names), High Walk, terrace,
  stairs, promenade, sea, street furniture, trees — `src/world/ground*`, `src/world/buildings*`.
- Vehicles (eight car styles, the bus, bicycles, scooters) and small props (birds, basketball,
  headphones, bag, fruit crate, kite, leaves…) — `src/world/vehicles*`, `src/world/props*`.
- The protagonist's wardrobe and skates, all skating animation, choreography, cameras, the cel/ink
  look and the freeze effect — `src/char/`, `src/shots/`, `src/engine/`.
- All textures are painted procedurally on canvases at load time; there are no image files besides
  the textures embedded in the avatar files.

## Software

Three.js (MIT) and @pixiv/three-vrm (MIT) for rendering and VRM support; Vite, TypeScript and
Playwright (driving headless Chrome on the local GPU) for the offline frame renderer; FFmpeg for
encoding. Blender is installed on the production machine and was permitted, but was not used.

## Fonts

The end title uses system fonts available to headless Chrome on Windows (Bahnschrift, Georgia).
No font files are distributed.
