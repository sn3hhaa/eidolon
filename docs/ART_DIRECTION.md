# EIDOLON — Art Direction

The seven images in `references/` are the visual authority for the film. They are
third-party artworks used locally as reference only and are **not** in this
repository (see [ASSET_CREDITS.md](ASSET_CREDITS.md)). This document records what was
read out of each one and the rules derived from them.

Every image was opened and inspected directly (six JPEGs, one GIF) on 2026-11-11.

---

## 1. Reference-by-reference breakdown

### R1 — Cosmic explosion → Scene 01 *The Awakening*, Scene 09 *The Return*
`724164815128581466.jpg` (736×414)

- **Composition.** A starburst slightly right of centre on pure black. Around it,
  torn irregular masses — part cloud, part continent, part ocean seen from orbit —
  break the frame into islands. Nothing is symmetrical; the black between the
  fragments is as much a shape as the fragments.
- **Burst.** A very small core: a bright ring with a hot orange heart. From it,
  hundreds of *needles* — thin, tapered, each a single saturated colour (orange,
  amber, magenta, cyan, acid green, violet, white). A dense fan of short
  yellow-white needles near the core, a sparser set of long ones reaching ~40 % of
  the frame, and loose dashes that have detached and float away.
- **Fragments.** Interiors are deep navy to near-black with soft brushy variation.
  Edges are thick cream-white impasto, lumpy like cumulus. Where a fragment faces
  the burst the cream turns gold, then burnt orange in its shadows.
- **Details.** Small four-point "+" sparkles in pink and cyan. Pale cratered ovals
  (moon-stones) at the left.
- **Lighting logic.** One light source. Everything is rim-lit by it; nothing is
  lit from the front. Space stays black right up to the light — there is no haze.

### R2 — Ancient gateway → Scene 02 *The Forgotten Gate*
`848858229807530269.jpg` (735×412)

- **Palette.** One dominant, almost flat **cobalt blue** sky (≈ `#0A2FA8` → `#06207A`)
  with a small white moon. Greens are grey-teal jade for the stone (`#4E8F6E`) and
  mid leaf-green for the foliage (`#3F8E4A`), all sitting on near-black shadow.
- **Gateway.** A tiered, heavily carved stone arch (South/Southeast-Asian temple
  vocabulary): stacked cornices, finials, carved flanking wings, a dark opening
  showing sky. Lit as flat tone + dark line, like a screen print.
- **Vegetation.** Giant lotus leaves as large flat discs with radial veins; lily
  pads on the water; clipped cloud-pruned trees with dark, clumped canopies; tiny
  white and red flowers as dots.
- **Figure.** A kneeling woman in profile, hands joined, black hair, dark robe with
  gold ornament — a silhouette with pattern, not a modelled body.
- **Water.** Dark blue mirror; reflections are vertical smears of the gate's
  columns.
- **Texture.** Flat colour planes, hard shape edges, fine dark outlines. Depth comes
  from overlap and value, not from fog.

### R3 — Giant flower forest → Scene 03 *The Impossible Forest*, Scene 08 *The Last Memory*
`b544fba45a4588c0ab8fc7d117fb8f8b.jpg` (1199×804)

- **Composition.** More than half the frame is **black**. A single enormous pink
  poppy-like flower on a tall white stalk dominates the upper right; a moss-covered
  serpentine trunk sweeps from lower right to centre; bare branching twigs reach
  into the black at left.
- **Flower.** Petals shade pale pink (`#F7B8C8`) to hot pink (`#F06A9A`) with
  lavender in the shadows; a white egg-like pistil in a crown of red stamens. The
  stalk is white-blue and flares into the petals like a mushroom's gills.
- **Secondary flora.** Pink chrysanthemum / protea pom-poms with spiky petals;
  blue-violet coral-like bushes; grey lichens.
- **Graphic intrusions.** Flat **vermilion vertical bars** (`#E8452C`) and a teal
  bar cut through the scene like torn paper or torii posts — pure graphic shapes
  that ignore the lighting.
- **Figure.** A small traveller seated by a campfire in a red robe with blue and
  white panels. The fire is the only warm light.
- **Ground.** Black water with flat patches of pale teal and violet and scattered
  red leaves.
- **Texture.** Airbrush-smooth gradients inside crisp inked shapes; moss rendered as
  dense stippling in yellow-green on dark green.

### R4 — Night garden → Scene 04 *The Garden of Stars*
`946178202976716466.gif` (735×490)

- **Composition.** A wall of flowers rising from the bottom edge to about two
  thirds of the frame, against a blue-black sky full of warm yellow points and
  fine diagonal rain streaks.
- **Flora inventory.** Blue and violet delphinium spires (left), red-orange
  poppies (lower left), a pink-red lily, cream and peach lilies (centre), white
  hydrangea clusters, blue irises, yellow day-lilies, white daisies with yellow
  centres (lower centre), magenta dahlias and pink cosmos (right), tall
  orange-pink wildflowers (upper right).
- **Texture.** Dense, wet, painterly; every petal has a light edge. Leaves are dark
  green with fine highlights. No empty ground anywhere.
- **Light.** The flowers appear self-lit; the sky gives nothing back.

### R5 — Celestial herd → Scene 05 *The Celestial Herd*
`6cba19e3cd6095f8679831eb623ce3f0.jpg` (1000×645)

- **Composition.** A lime-green hill fills two thirds of the frame under a black
  sky. Above it floats a large crescent-and-disc being; below it, a dark pit
  framed by a rainbow arch.
- **The being.** A pale cream crescent wrapped around an **orange disc** built from
  concentric rings, with a red-and-black eye at its centre and a small sleepy face
  (two lashes, a red mouth) at the lower edge.
- **Creatures.** White long-horned antelopes with red halo rings and pale red body
  markings, each with a warm outline glow. Some stand on the hill, others leap
  through the sky in an arc. Soft white humanoid figures stand around the pit.
- **Portal.** A black oval in the grass ringed with tiny white flowers; inside, a
  full rainbow arch, shooting stars and small symbols; a tiny Earth sits on its
  rim.
- **Grass.** Hand-drawn hatching in lime (`#7FD13B`), yellow-green and darker
  green tufts, with patches of pale blue. The hilltop edge is drawn in rust-brown
  blades against the black.
- **Texture.** Coloured-pencil / digital-crayon line over flat fills. Deliberately
  naive.

### R6 — Midnight carnival → Scene 06 *The Midnight Carnival*
`Who is The ARTIST 🎸, this is amazing_.jpg` (768×368)

- **Style.** Hand-placed **pixel art** with heavy dithering — every gradient is a
  stepped ramp. This is the one reference whose surface is pixel-based rather than
  painted.
- **Composition.** Symmetrical stage set: a central purple fortune-teller's tent
  with a hooded figure and crystal ball; red-orange striped show tents either side;
  behind them a Ferris wheel outlined in cyan lights, a green moon-like balloon,
  a huge jack-o'-lantern, a roller-coaster; a painted witch signboard at right;
  golden heart balloons at left; overhanging tree foliage in the top corners.
- **Floor.** A blue checkerboard in forced perspective with a red carpet running
  to the central tent.
- **Light.** Theatrical and coloured: purple and cyan on the centre, hot
  red-orange on the flanks, green on the balloon. Shadows go to pure black.
- **Reinterpretation note.** The film borrows this image's *language* — stage-set
  symmetry, checkerboard perspective, stepped colour ramps, coloured theatrical
  light — and builds an original carnival from it. Specific tents, signs and
  characters from the reference are not reproduced.

### R7 — Flying city → Scene 07 *The Floating City*
`🌙en 🏴_☠️ quantumspirit_eth_tez 🌈 (@JenPanepinto) on X.jpg` (735×547)

- **Style.** Hyper-detailed 1980s Japanese commercial illustration: airbrushed,
  glossy, saturated, with scale collisions everywhere.
- **Composition.** Horizontal bands. Bottom: a strip of framed posters. Middle: a
  pale platform of white, blue and cream where pedestrians and orange-suited
  workers walk. Above: a black sky crowded with a bulbous orange-and-cream aircraft
  with teal pontoons and passengers at the windows, small rockets and satellites,
  pink neon capsule signs with invented glyphs, a huge red coral-like sculpture,
  and — enormous, behind everything — a child's face whose skin is clouds and sea.
- **Palette.** Orange, cobalt, cream, vermilion, teal against black.
- **Reinterpretation note.** No real persons, logos or legible text are
  reproduced; signage uses invented glyphs, and the giant face becomes an original
  cloud-and-sea visage.

## 2. The shared visual identity

What the seven images have in common, and therefore what the film must hold onto
in every scene:

1. **Black is a colour.** Five of seven references sit on pure black. Skies and
   voids are `#000000`, not dark grey, not fogged. Bloom and haze must never lift
   it.
2. **Saturated colour in flat, bounded shapes.** Colour lives inside crisp
   silhouettes. Shading is 2–4 steps, not a continuous gradient.
3. **Density at the edges, emptiness in the middle distance.** Detail piles up in
   vegetation, ornament and crowds; then there is nothing.
4. **Light comes from objects.** A moon, a fire, a flower, a sign. There is no
   ambient fill and no sky light.
5. **Wrong scale.** Flowers taller than people, a face larger than a city, an eye
   the size of a hill.
6. **Hand-made surface.** Gouache, airbrush, crayon, pixel. Never plastic, never
   PBR-glossy, never "default material".

## 3. Master palette

| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#000000` | sky, negative space, deepest shadow |
| Deep navy | `#04071A` → `#0A1C5A` | fragment interiors, night water |
| Cobalt | `#0A2FA8` | the gate sky; secondary accents |
| Cream | `#F5E7C2` | moon, cloud rims, creatures, highlights |
| Gold | `#FF9A1A` | burst-lit edges, fire, carnival bulbs |
| Vermilion | `#E8452C` | bars, robes, tents, coral |
| Hot pink | `#F06A9A` / pale `#F7B8C8` | giant flowers, neon |
| Magenta | `#FF1F80` | needles, sparkles, signage |
| Cyan | `#14CCFF` | needles, Ferris wheel, glass |
| Acid green | `#7FD13B` | the hill; needles; carnival balloon |
| Jade | `#4E8F6E` | gateway stone |
| Violet | `#8542FF` | shadows of pink, carnival tent, needles |

The seven accent colours (orange, amber, magenta, cyan, acid, violet, cream) are
defined once in `src/engine/glsl.ts` (`eidolonAccent`) and reused by every light
effect so the film's "electric" colours stay identical from scene to scene.

## 4. Lighting

- One key source per scene, always visible or clearly implied; it should be
  possible to point at it.
- Rim-first: edges facing the source get the light, faces away from it go to
  black. No fill light, no ambient term above ~3 %.
- Warm/cool pairing from R1 and R3: light is cream-to-gold, shadow is
  navy-to-violet.
- Bloom is a seasoning. Threshold ≥ 1.0 (HDR), strength ≤ ~0.4. If bloom lifts the
  black level, it is wrong.

## 5. Texture philosophy

- **Paint in the shader.** Surfaces get directional brush noise, soft-edged tonal
  washes and stepped (cel) shading rather than photographic textures.
- **No hard quantisation of smooth fields.** Stepping a noise field into bands
  produces contour lines that read as a topographic map, not paint (see the
  experiment log, entry 1). Break wash edges with bristle noise instead.
- **Analytic normals for edge lighting.** Screen-space derivatives are evaluated per
  2×2 pixel quad and make rim light blocky.
- **A print surface on top.** A static paper tooth and a light per-frame grain are
  applied in the final pass and scale with luminance, so black stays black.
- **Kuwahara filter** (`painterly` look parameter) is available per scene to melt
  rendered geometry into dabs. It is off in Scene 01 because it erases one-pixel
  needles; it is intended for the botanical scenes.

## 6. Environment design rules

- Compose in three planes: a dark foreground frame, a lit subject plane, a sparse
  far plane. Leave real black between them.
- Vegetation is built from large readable shapes first (discs, spires, pom-poms),
  detail second.
- Architecture is frontal and ornamental, read as silhouette + line.
- Every scene needs one object at an impossible scale.

## 7. Character design rules

- Figures are small, still and seen in profile or from behind. They are
  silhouettes carrying pattern, not modelled anatomy.
- No faces in close-up. No dialogue, no lip movement.
- Creatures (R5) are white, outlined, haloed, and move slowly.
- The cloaked figure of Scene 02 and the traveller of Scenes 03/08 are the same
  person: dark hair, patterned robe, always turned away from the camera.

## 8. Consistency rules (the checklist each scene is judged against)

1. Is the black actually black? (Sample a corner: it should be < 3 % luminance.)
2. Can you name the light source?
3. Does at least half the palette come from the master table?
4. Is there a shape that is the wrong size?
5. Is there anything that looks like a default sphere, cube or `MeshStandardMaterial`?
   If yes, it is a placeholder and must be listed in `ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.
6. Does a still frame look like a page from an illustrated book, or like a tech demo?
